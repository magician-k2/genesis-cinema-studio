# 🧠 GENESIS & Google AI Studio Development Master Guidelines

## 🌟 Permanent Framework: Google AI Studio 7-Step App Builder
Whenever the user asks to build, architect, upgrade, or deploy an application, always follow the **Google AI Studio 7-Step Production App Builder Framework**:

1. **Step 1: Architecture & Spec (One-Prompt Blueprint)**:
   - Establish complete technical spec, modern tech stack (React/Tailwind/FastAPI), and module breakdowns before writing code.
2. **Step 2: MVP Step-by-Step (Zero Placeholders)**:
   - Deliver 100% complete, runnable, production code. NEVER use stub comments like // TODO or placeholder buttons.
3. **Step 3: Level Up UI/UX (Premium SaaS Aesthetics)**:
   - Apply Linear/Apple-grade visual polish: glassmorphism, refined micro-padding, tactile borders, smooth transitions, dark mode default, and fluid responsiveness.
4. **Step 4: Auth, Database & Storage (Data Layer)**:
   - Provide robust schemas, secure session management, protected routes, and type-safe CRUD operations.
5. **Step 5: Turn into an AI Product (Gemini & Edge Engine)**:
   - Integrate Google AI Studio (Gemini 2.5/3.x) & on-device Gemma 4 with streaming responses, JSON schemas, retry logic, and low perceived latency.
6. **Step 6: Stress Test & Ruthless QA (Adversarial Audit)**:
   - Act as an elitist, uncompromising QA and security auditor. Eliminate race conditions, broken edge cases, and rate limit risks.
7. **Step 7: Launch Readiness & Monetization**:
   - Deliver one-click cloud deployment configurations (Cloud Run / Replit / Vercel), analytics tracking, and monetization readiness.
